I believe that sharing a meal is the most honest way to bring people together. This is my invitation to pull up a chair.

Kelyne Noel

My roots are Bamiléké. My grandmother is from Baham, in Cameroon, and that's where this food starts for me.

À Bonne Table is how I share it here in Raleigh: at cook-alongs, around dinner tables, in classes, and at market tables. The classics stay the classics, and I also cook plant-forward versions that keep the flavor where it belongs.
